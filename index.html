The Lord of the Low Places

Weles keeps the underworld the way a good farmer keeps a barn: warm, orderly, and full of things that belong to him. He is the god of cattle and of wealth, of oaths and of magic, of poets and of other professional liars.

He does not ask for war. He does not ask for weather. He asks that accounts be kept, that words be kept, and that the thunder be politely ignored.

The Church of Slyboots is his ledger on earth. It is a small operation. He prefers it that way.

Questions Put to the Church

Is this a real church?

The domain is real. The Ledger is real. The rest is between you and Weles.

What is required of an apostle?

Very little. Weles favors the economical. An epistle will arrive when there is need. There are no meetings.

Czy muszę mówić po polsku?

No. The liturgy is Polish because the liturgy is old. The Church is patient with newcomers.

Why is it called Slyboots?

An old English word for a quiet schemer. The god reviewed the translation and found it fair.

Can I leave?

Yes. Write to the Church and your page is torn from the Ledger. The serpent does not chase.
